Why Insurance Matters

Your Health Can't Wait —
Neither Should Your Coverage

Medical emergencies rarely announce themselves. A cardiac event, a complex surgery, or a prolonged ICU stay can cost lakhs within days. Health insurance is not a luxury — it is your most essential financial safety net.

At MNR Hospital, we believe financial barriers should never stand between a patient and the care they need. That is why we have invested deeply in empanelling with India's leading insurance providers and building a dedicated insurance facilitation team that works solely to make your hospitalisation stress-free.

Cashless Admission

Pre-authorised cashless treatment from Day 1 — no deposit, no upfront payment for covered procedures.

Paperwork Handled for You

Our insurance team manages all claim documentation, pre-authorisation requests, and insurer coordination on your behalf.

Fast Authorisation

Average pre-authorisation turnaround of under 3 hours for elective procedures and immediate for emergencies.

Dedicated Insurance Desk

A dedicated helpdesk at the hospital — available 7 days a week to guide patients through every step of the claim process.

Process

How Cashless Treatment Works

A simple, guided process — from admission to discharge — entirely managed by our insurance facilitation team.

Present Your Card

Show your insurance card or policy details at the MNR Hospital Insurance Desk upon arrival.

Pre-Authorisation

Our team submits a pre-authorisation request to your insurer. Average approval time: under 3 hours.

Receive Treatment

Focus entirely on your treatment. All billing and documentation is handled between us and your insurer.

Cashless Discharge

Walk out at discharge without paying for covered procedures. We settle directly with your insurer.

Government Schemes

Empanelled Under National Schemes

MNR Hospital ensures that government health schemes are accessible for all eligible patients without any additional hassle.

Ayushman Bharat – PMJAY

India's largest health protection scheme covering ₹5 lakhs per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalisation. No premium, no limit on family size.

MNR Hospital Empanelled

ESIC – Employees' State Insurance

Comprehensive medical care for ESI-covered employees and their dependents. Includes specialist consultations, hospitalisation, surgery, and maternity benefits.

ESI Empanelled Hospital

Karnataka Arogya Bhagya

State government scheme providing healthcare benefits to BPL and APL families of Karnataka. Covers a wide range of procedures at empanelled hospitals like MNR Hospital.
