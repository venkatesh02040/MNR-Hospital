Who We Are

A New Standard of Healthcare
for Coastal Karnataka

MNR Hospital is a purpose-built, NABH-accredited multi-specialty hospital at Kolambe, Bajpe minutes from Mangalore International Airport. We were built with a clear goal: to eliminate the need for patients across Mangalore, Udupi, and Kasaragod to travel to distant cities for quality care.

From state-of-the-art operation theatres and advanced diagnostic imaging to a dedicated Level III NICU and round-the-clock emergency services every inch of MNR Hospital is designed around the patient experience.

Modern infrastructure the latest medical technology and patient-first facility design.

Expert panel of doctors 50+ specialists across 7 departments, many trained at top institutes across India.

Affordable & transparent Ayushman Bharat empanelled, insurance-friendly, zero hidden billing.

Community focused regular free health camps, rural outreach, and awareness programs.

Why MNR Hospital

What Sets Us Apart

We are not just another hospital. We are a carefully designed healthcare ecosystem built around what patients actually need.

Advanced Diagnostic Technology

MRI, 128-slice CT, digital mammography, PET-CT, and a fully automated pathology lab — accurate results, fast.

Modern Operation Theatres

Laminar-flow OTs with laparoscopic and minimally invasive surgery capability, reducing recovery times significantly.

Multi-Level ICU & Critical Care

Dedicated MICU, SICU, CICU, and Neonatal ICU with 24/7 intensivists and advanced ventilator support.

Transparent, Affordable Billing

No surprise charges. Ayushman Bharat empanelled, major insurance accepted, and a dedicated patient counselling desk.

Beyond the Hospital

Giving Back to Our Community

Healthcare doesn't stop at our doors. We actively reach out to communities through outreach programs, free camps, and awareness initiatives.

Free Health Camps

Regular camps in rural areas of Dakshina Kannada and Udupi offering free consultation, BP checks, diabetes screening, and basic diagnostics.

Health Awareness Drives

School and college programs on women's health, mental wellness, nutrition, and preventive care — reaching thousands each year.

Support for the Underprivileged

Ayushman Bharat empanelled and tied up with multiple state schemes so financial constraints never become a barrier to essential care.
