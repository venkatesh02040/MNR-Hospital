Why MNR Hospital

World-Class Treatment at
Indian Value

At MNR Hospital, we understand that travelling abroad for medical treatment is a significant decision. That is why we have built an entire ecosystem to make your journey from the first inquiry to full recovery seamless, safe, and transparent.

Located minutes from Mangalore International Airport with direct flights from the Middle East, Gulf, and major Indian metros, MNR Hospital is exceptionally accessible for international patients.

Multilingual coordinators available in Arabic, English, Kannada, Hindi, and Malayalam.

End-to-end assistance visa support, airport pickup, accommodation, and post-discharge follow-up.
