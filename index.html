FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions patients ask before visiting MNR Hospital.

Do I need a prior appointment to visit the OPD?

Walk-ins are welcome at our OPD during working hours. However, booking an appointment online or by phone ensures you are attended to faster and reduces waiting time, especially for specialist consultations.

What documents should I bring for my first visit?

Please bring a government-issued photo ID, any previous medical records, lab reports, imaging scans, and a list of current medications. This helps our doctors understand your medical history immediately.

Does MNR Hospital accept insurance?

Yes, we are empanelled with most major insurance providers and Third Party Administrators (TPAs). Please contact us with your insurer's name to confirm coverage before your visit. We also accept Ayushman Bharat.

How do I reach MNR Hospital from Mangalore city?

We are located at Kolambe, behind Bajpe Airport on the NH-66. From Mangalore city centre it is approximately 12 km. Auto-rickshaws, taxis, and city buses are available. Free parking is available on campus.

What is the emergency contact number?

Our emergency and casualty department is reachable 24 hours a day, 7 days a week at 9876543210. We are equipped for cardiac emergencies, trauma, and critical care round the clock.

Can I get a second opinion at MNR Hospital?

Absolutely. Our senior specialists are available for second opinion consultations. Please bring all existing reports, imaging, and a referral if available. Select "Second Opinion" when booking your appointment.
