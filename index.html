What We Offer

Comprehensive Hospital Services

Everything you need for complete healthcare — under one roof, 24 hours a day.

Emergency & Trauma Care

24-hour emergency department with fully equipped trauma bays, rapid triage, and specialist response teams on call.

Advanced Diagnostics

State-of-the-art lab, MRI, CT Scan, PET-CT, digital mammography, and fully automated pathology services.

Surgical Services

Laparoscopic, robotic-assisted, and open surgical procedures with dedicated recovery wards across all specialties.

Maternity & NICU

Comprehensive maternity care with a Level III NICU for premature and high-risk newborns, round the clock.

ICU & Critical Care

Dedicated MICU, SICU, and CICU with advanced ventilator support and 24/7 intensivist coverage.

Pharmacy & Blood Bank

24/7 in-house pharmacy with a full-service blood bank, component separation, and on-site storage facilities.
