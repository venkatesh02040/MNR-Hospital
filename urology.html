About the Department

Precision Care for the Urinary System

Urology is the surgical specialty concerned with the urinary tract in both men and women, and the male reproductive system. Its scope is remarkably broad: from the kidneys and ureters to the bladder, urethra, prostate, and testes. Urological conditions affect a significant proportion of the population — kidney stones are among the most common causes of acute pain, benign prostatic hyperplasia affects the majority of men over 60, and urological cancers (prostate, bladder, renal cell) are among the most prevalent cancers worldwide.

Modern urology has been revolutionised by endoscopic and laparoscopic techniques. Where patients once needed open surgery for kidney stones or prostate conditions, today the same outcomes are achieved through a small camera and laser — with recovery measured in days rather than weeks. Robotic-assisted surgery, flexible ureteroscopy, and holmium laser technology have made urological procedures safer, more precise, and far less disruptive to patients' lives.

Urology at MNR Hospital

The Department of Urology at MNR Hospital is equipped with dedicated endoscopic and laparoscopic operating capability, including a holmium laser system for precision kidney stone fragmentation. Our urologists manage the full spectrum of urological conditions: acute renal colic, recurrent stone disease, LUTS from benign prostatic obstruction, bladder dysfunction, urological malignancies, and complex male reproductive concerns. We operate a structured uro-oncology pathway in collaboration with our oncology consultants for the evaluation and staging of prostate, bladder, and renal cancers.

Our diagnostic capability includes flexible cystoscopy, urodynamics, renal ultrasonography, and high-resolution CT urography. Surgical services include TURP, TURBT, PCNL, URSL (ureteroscopy with laser lithotripsy), and laparoscopic pyeloplasty, nephrectomy, and adrenalectomy. Our andrology clinic provides a confidential and comprehensive evaluation of male infertility, erectile dysfunction, and hormonal concerns — an area of care that remains underserved across the region.

Restoring Quality of Life Through Expert Urological Care

Many urological conditions — frequent urination at night, difficulty passing urine, recurring infections, or blood in urine — are dismissed as normal ageing or embarrassment. They are not. They are treatable, and treatment dramatically improves quality of life. At MNR Hospital, we encourage every patient to speak openly about urological concerns in a consultation that is confidential, respectful, and free of judgement. Our team is committed to the most effective and minimally invasive solutions — because getting back to normal life, as quickly as possible, is always the goal.

Conditions We Treat

Common Urology Conditions

Kidney Stones

Calcium or uric acid stones causing severe colicky pain.

Benign Prostatic Hyperplasia

Enlarged prostate causing urinary obstruction in men.

Bladder Disorders

Overactive bladder, interstitial cystitis, and incontinence.

Prostate Cancer

The most common cancer in men — highly treatable when caught early.

Recurrent UTIs

Frequent urinary tract infections requiring specialist evaluation.

Urinary Strictures

Narrowing of the urethra causing obstructed urine flow.

Male Infertility

Azoospermia, varicocele, and hormonal causes of infertility.

Bladder & Renal Cancer

Haematuria investigations and oncological staging.
